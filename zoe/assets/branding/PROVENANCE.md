# ZOE visual identity provenance

`zoe-eye.{png,svg}` is the primary ZOE emblem, generated by `assets/branding/render_eye.py` from the user-supplied eye/orbital reference. Its central Z, nested eye curves, stars, and numbered nodes are an original composition, not a scientific model or a copied source image.

`orbital-emblem.png` is the user-supplied image `Screenshot From 2026-11-05 02-35-39.png`. It established the shared orbital motif. The unified hub renders a vector interpretation using the Spatial IR scene in `guiai/gem/unified.py`.

The small symbol-grid references in `references/` are also user-supplied screenshots. They guide the outlined G, E, and M cells in the native scene. Their labels are visual references, not claims about the original font's semantics or a new expansion of the GEM name.

- `symbol-z.png`: `Screenshot From 2026-11-05 01-59-40.png`
- `symbol-o.png`: `Screenshot From 2026-11-05 02-00-04.png`
- `symbol-epsilon.png`: `Screenshot From 2026-11-05 02-00-16.png`
- `symbol-g.png`: `Screenshot From 2026-11-05 02-00-49.png`
- `symbol-e.png`: `Screenshot From 2026-11-05 02-00-56.png`
- `symbol-m.png`: `Screenshot From 2026-11-05 02-01-07.png`

The reference images remain distinct from generated GEM scenes. `guiai-lab/docs/images/gem-unified.{png,svg,gem}` is generated executable Spatial IR. `gem-unified-native.png` is an actual nested-X11 GTK screenshot. Earlier Cognitive Field and anatomical artwork retain their own provenance under `guiai-lab/docs/references/cognitive-field/`.
